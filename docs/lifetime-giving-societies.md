# Lifetime Giving Societies: History Review

## Scope

Reporting > Dashboards > Lifetime Giving Societies uses Gift query **31118**, **Lifetime Giving - JUMGOGPT**. It is an initial history-review report, not a complete reconstruction of complex recognition histories or an official society induction register.

The report imports a saved, searchable constituent history, checks both society thresholds, provides provisional dates for uncomplicated direct and approved full-recognition gifts, and exports filtered results to CSV. Staff can explicitly verify bounded linked pledge/payment/write-off history to include reconciled recognition. Records still needing reconciliation remain visible without a guessed date or complete total; a separately labeled supported-gifts subtotal shows only supported history. No NXT gift, constituent, recognition, appeal or custom-field records are written. Household confirmation is report-only and does not change existing society eligibility logic elsewhere.

The saved active society configurations supply thresholds and count sources. Following the user's November 16 clarification, this report explicitly includes recognition credit for **1934 Club**, in addition to its saved committed-giving source. Frances Bartlett Kinne Society already includes both sources. Current thresholds are $100,000 and $1,000,000 respectively. The user additionally approved **Full amount to all** recognition, following query 31118 rather than recipient allocation amounts. This is a report-specific rule, not a write to shared society configuration or NXT. Other eligibility workflows are unchanged. Rule version 5 invalidates earlier calculations and company/person confirmations until a refresh; rule/configuration changes are checked during publication.

## Married Households And Credit Sources

Two individuals are grouped automatically when their query output contains reciprocal spouse IDs, both have a consistent Married marital status, and no other constituent claims either spouse. Per the user's clarification, `Alum married to alum` is equivalent to `Married`; case and surrounding whitespace are ignored, and those two labels across repeated rows are not a conflict. Both labels normalize to the same spouse evidence so label changes alone do not undo staff separation. IDs must be in the same namespace: exact Constituent IDs or verified system IDs, never names or integration IDs. Pairs do not need shared gifts. Both must be present as individuals in the imported query history. Blank, invalid, one-sided, inconsistent, self, organization and competing links remain separate with review notes. Missing, divorced, separated, widowed or unknown marital status is not assumed to mean Married. Current spouse links do not establish the historical date of a marriage; the report continues to describe calculated milestone dates as provisional.

Organizations are credit sources, never automatic household members. A normal household combines only gifts given by or explicitly credited to its individual members, deduplicated by gift system ID. For example, two spouses credited for one corporate gift receive that gift once as a household; other gifts from the company do not enter their household. The company remains a separate report record unless explicitly linked in a family giving group below. A current spouse pair's combined history includes their separate personal gifts as well as shared gifts, not just the shared-gift count displayed on the card.

Exact co-credit sets of two or more remaining individuals can suggest a household for explicit Admin/Advancement Services confirmation. Companies are excluded before these suggestions are made. A+B on one gift and B+C on another never automatically create A+B+C. Individuals already in automatic households or with unresolved spouse links are not suggested for another household. Neither shared gifts nor names prove marriage. No excluded spouse, estate, foundation or other record is silently added. Server-side validation rejects organization membership and overlap with another active household.

The default view combines automatic and manually confirmed households and any explicit family groups. A local toggle restores separate records without changing saved grouping. Admins/Advancement Services can also keep an automatic spouse pair separate or restore it with an explicit confirmation. Household rows appear in the individuals view; separate companies appear in the organizations view and mixed family groups have their own filter. Search and CSV include member IDs and grouping basis without inventing an NXT household ID. Recognition totals must not be added together as organizational revenue.

Confirmation, undo and staff separation use the current revision, compare-and-swap publication and a private bounded audit trail. They do not execute NXT queries. Refresh retains manual confirmation or staff separation only when rule version, rule/source fingerprints, member identities and the relevant spouse/shared-gift evidence still match. Staff separation depends on spouse evidence, not the gift list: new gifts alone do not restore automatic grouping. Old v4 company/person groups are not carried forward. These are snapshot-scoped decisions, not a canonical crosswalk. Manual suggestions are bounded to 1,000 sets with at most 20 members each; automatic pairs are bounded by the 10,000-person import limit, not the suggestion cap. All separate histories remain available.

## Explicit Family Giving Groups

Admins and Advancement Services can use **Link foundation** on an active household, **Link to household** on an organization, or **Family giving groups > Link foundation to household**. Choose the exact saved household and organization, then explicitly acknowledge including **all imported qualifying organization gifts**, including gifts not soft credited to either spouse. No organization is added by name, co-credit, or automatic spouse logic. Cancel restores the originating control without saving. Linking combines saved evidence only, without a query refresh or any NXT request/write.

The distinct family group contains the household's individual histories plus every explicitly linked organization's qualifying imported history, once per gift system ID. Its gift details, local filters, counts and CSV use the same scope. Separate constituent and spouse histories stay unchanged and remain available using the separate-record toggle. These recognition totals are not additive revenue. Existing query exclusions still apply; the link does not fetch excluded or missing foundation gifts. Complex histories still need explicit reconciliation before their dates can be confirmed.

Family dates have their own keys. Existing constituent, organization and spouse-household date confirmations are retained but never copied to the family. Changing its exact membership creates a new scope needing its own review; returning to an identical prior scope can restore that scope's saved confirmation. Confirmed family dates survive refresh without recalculating their threshold crossing. Archived foundation credit remains in an active family. The optional linked-history check verifies the family scope separately and does not copy another scope's accepted evidence.

Durable `familyLinks` bind exact household and organization record keys. A successful refresh rebuilds a group only when every member and its active underlying household still matches. Missing, renamed, retyped or changed-namespace identities, changed spouse membership, and missing member evidence leave the saved link visibly unmatched; the group never silently shrinks. Staff can unlink an unmatched choice even while report rules are stale. An organization can belong to only one saved family, with at most ten organizations per household and 1,000 links overall. Underlying household changes require unlinking its organizations first. UI link lists are searchable and paginated.

`link-family` accepts only current revision, household key and organization record key; `unlink-family` accepts current revision and saved link key. Both use role/origin checks, a second rules check, atomic compare-and-swap, and a private 200-event audit. Public responses omit actors and evidence. No schema migration, NXT merge, live backfill, deployment-time link or automatic confirmation occurs.

## Confirmed Dates And Archived Records

Opening/filtering the report reads saved results only. Admins and Advancement Services can explicitly **Confirm entry date** for a current, active recipient/society with a supported provisional date. The server saves its own date and gift references, never a client-supplied date or amount. Unresolved histories cannot be confirmed. This is a staff-confirmed report date, not an NXT induction record. Confirmed dates do not change on later refreshes; their chronological threshold-crossing calculation is skipped. Gift identity, duplication, credit, conflict and amount checks still run, and history totals/warnings can change. A later society-rule change is flagged without silently changing the confirmed date.

**Reopen date review** explicitly removes one society's lock and records the prior decision in the private audit. If a refresh skipped that calculation, the date becomes undated/needs-review until another explicit gift-history refresh recalculates it. Other society dates are unaffected. No background recalculation or NXT write occurs on confirmation/reopening.

**Archive record** hides one individual or organization from active separate-record views in both societies, not from the gift source or calculations. For example, archiving a matching-gift company retains every gift explicitly credited to a kept individual and the individual's household. Unrelated company gifts never enter their history. Archived owners still participate in conflict/deduplication checks. Archive choices are reversible via **Archived records**, **Active & archived**, or **Saved dates & archived records**. The CSV follows the visibility filter and labels confirmed dates, archive status and retained household credit. Nothing changes in NXT or other app reports/society eligibility.

An active household remains visible while at least one member is active and retains all members' credit; archived members are labeled in its member details. When all members are archived, the household leaves the active view. Archived view always lists separate constituents so each can be restored. Archiving a constituent never partially recalculates a household or transfers its confirmed date to the remaining member.

Decisions live in `recordDecisions`, outside the replaceable `snapshot` in the existing report state, and survive successful refreshes even when a record leaves the query. Keys bind to query-local identifier namespace, exact ID, name, record type, and available system/lookup IDs. Household dates additionally bind to grouping basis and the exact member identities, not transient shared-gift evidence hashes. New gifts/output aliases do not discard decisions. Changed identity details, changed household membership, or a switch of identifier namespace do not transfer decisions by guesswork: unmatched decisions remain visible separately for explicit restore/reopen. These are not canonical NXT identity mappings. Staff should review unmatched choices after source identity changes.

All changes use the current revision and atomic compare-and-swap, including races with refresh/group changes. A private bounded 500-event decision audit retains actors and prior values; public responses omit actors/audit. Saved choices are bounded to 20,000 archive records and 40,000 milestones, with no silent truncation. No schema migration, production backfill, automatic confirmation or automatic archiving is required.

## Views And Filters

### Review Workspace

The working view defaults to **Needs attention**: unconfirmed milestones plus confirmed dates with current calculation/rule warnings. **Ready to confirm**, **Needs investigation**, **Confirmed**, and **All history** are local queue choices. Counts honor the selected society, recipient, year, archive and household filters and count milestone checks, not unique society members. A confirmed date with a warning can appear in both Confirmed and Needs investigation; its lock is never removed automatically. Archive views initially show all statuses so previously confirmed archived records remain easy to restore. CSV still exports the entire filtered view, not only the visible page.

**Review gift details** opens an accessible, focused panel and returns to the same filtered list without a new source refresh. It shows specific next steps, gift system IDs, dates, whole-gift amounts, types, direct/soft/matching credit and supported/excluded/reconciliation outcomes. Supported means included in the supported subtotal, not a complete lifetime total or confirmed milestone when other history is unresolved. Conflicting values are withheld, not displayed as a trusted amount. Same-gift credit claims are shown together and counted at most once for the recipient/household. Household evidence includes only its actual individual members; archived source organizations' explicitly credited gifts remain, and unrelated company gifts do not enter the panel. No within-day order or historical running balance is invented.

Calculation and explanation use the same gift classification and verified linked-history helpers. Duplicate conflicts are propagated to all known credited recipients even if a later duplicate happens to match one of them. Linked checks are opt-in, not automatically triggered by opening a report or gift panel.

### Reconciliation Checklist And Export

The gift panel has a collapsed **Reconciliation checklist** with whole-recipient counts and specific evidence requests for pledges, pledge payments, company matching gifts, dated write-offs/adjustments, planned gifts, stock/property proceeds, recurring commitments, and data/credit conflicts. Each unresolved gift also has **Evidence needed for this gift**. A gift can require several checks; category counts are not additive. Supported gifts and excluded employee matching credit are not turned into new reconciliation tasks.

**Export all unresolved gifts (CSV)** downloads every unresolved distinct gift for the selected recipient/active household and society, independent of the visible page/filter. It includes gift system IDs, source dates/types/amounts, exact credited recipients, evidence requirements, snapshot time and the unchanged staff-confirmed date when present. Shared household gifts appear once. Unrelated company gifts and excluded credits are omitted; archiving the credit source does not remove a kept individual's explicit credit. Conflicting values stay blank. Amounts are prominently labeled non-additive, and the file is explicitly not an NXT import template or a completed reconciliation.

This is a saved-data GET with `milestoneKey`, `revision`, and `format=reconciliation-csv`; page/view parameters are rejected for exports to avoid implying a partial file is complete. It uses the same authorization, current-rule/revision and active-household checks as gift details. Responses are private/no-store CSV attachments with formula-safe cells and a 32 MiB UTF-8 size limit checked during serialization; oversized files fail without truncation. Failed exports do not download error/login pages. Closing the panel aborts pending downloads. No NXT requests, record mutations, rule-version change, migration or refresh are needed for existing version-1 evidence ledgers.

The checklist/export prepares evidence for unresolved cases. The linked-history check below can resolve supported pledge cases, but never infers links from names, equal amounts, a current balance or nearby dates. The separate campaign pledge checker is unchanged; it is not reused for historical society recognition.

### Explicit Linked-History Checks

From a saved gift's details, Admins and Advancement Services can choose **Check linked history**. It follows only returned gift links, pledge installment applications and recipient system IDs through allowlisted GET endpoints. It never executes the source query or changes NXT. Five serial GETs are checkpointed per request; the UI continues while open, with **Pause after current step** and explicit continuation/restart. Opening the page never starts or resumes checks. Checks use the shared reporting connection, existing quota/cooldown behavior and 8-second read timeouts without automatic per-read retries.

Each job is bounded to 80 gifts, 150 evidence paths, 300 reads, 12 reconciled pledges and 30 minutes. Installment/application collections are capped at 1,000 entries, with completeness checks and no truncation. Every collected object is read again before publication; structural comparison tolerates JSONB key ordering, not changed evidence. All applications must reconcile individually to installment amounts/balances and whole-pledge totals. Unsupported types, explicit adjustments/amendments, mismatched query amounts/dates/types/identities, missing links, duplicate or incomplete applications, oversized graphs and concurrent rule/snapshot changes fail closed. Current API evidence does not prove that all historical amendments are available; dates remain provisional until staff confirms.

The saved query establishes which recipients qualify for recognition. Live owner/soft-credit system IDs are resolved only through the returned constituent profiles and matched exactly to the query's namespaced IDs. No identity search or name matching is used. Full-gift credit applies to explicitly eligible recipients; excluded employee matching flags are not converted to credit. A payment absent from the recipient's query history can reconcile a balance but does not gain recognition. Original donor gifts linked to matching gifts remain separate contributions and are also validated against the saved query. Company giving enters a household only through explicit member credit; archive choices never remove retained credit.

An earlier payment legitimately applied to a later pledge retains its original recognition date. The later credited pledge adds only the commitment not already recognized through those earlier payments. Later payments do not add amounts already recognized through their credited pledges. Amounts applied to multiple pledges and unapplied remainders are accounted for once. A recipient credited only for the payment does not receive credit backdated to someone else's pledge. Verified dated write-offs reduce credited pledges even when omitted from the recipient query; zero balance is not treated as proof of payment. Same-day signed events are netted before threshold testing. If a later reduction takes an unconfirmed crossing below its threshold, the date stays in staff review even if later giving crosses again. Confirmed dates remain locked.

Private `snapshot.giftReview.linkedScopes` stores resumable jobs and at most 50 accepted graphs per exact recipient/household scope within the existing 32 MiB ledger limit. Only that scope's two society rows are recalculated on completion. Contradictory rechecks invalidate overlapping accepted graphs and withhold unconfirmed dates; affected gifts also leave the supported subtotal. Persisted review blockers clear only after a successful check covers all affected gift IDs, not just the initial seed. Changed links that leave unresolved evidence outside the new graph can require a source refresh. Connection failures pause with progress saved. Confirmed decisions, other recipients and the separate campaign dashboard are not changed. Public responses expose only sanitized progress and derived explanation, not raw profiles/provider evidence. Publication checks the current revision and rules and records a bounded private audit. A successful source refresh replaces this optional evidence and safely requires fresh linked checks for unconfirmed complex histories; date locks survive.

An explicit history refresh saves a compact private `snapshot.giftReview` ledger (version 1, maximum 32 MiB, no silent truncation). Standard summary GETs exclude that ledger in SQL and public summary/mutation responses always strip it. The existing report GET accepts validated `milestoneKey`, current `revision`, zero-based `page` and `view` parameters to return at most 25 distinct gift explanations. Access is rechecked for every request; no NXT transport is initialized. Stale source/rules/revisions or changed household membership reject the read. Failed/aborted reads never replace the selected page with another recipient's data, and access revocation removes report data from the UI.

Older valid snapshots remain usable without a forced rule-version migration. Their gift panel explains that details were not saved and asks an Admin/Advancement Services user to refresh once if desired. Nothing refreshes automatically. Confirmed dates, archive decisions, grouping and write permissions retain their existing contracts. No live backfill, schema change or source-output change is required.

Prominent society buttons select Both societies, 1934 Club, or Frances Bartlett Kinne Society. Constituent type selects individuals, organizations, or their combined view. Search, review status, and calculated fiscal year further narrow the view. Counts and CSV exports use all filtered rows, not just the visible page; constituent counts deduplicate the two society rows. These controls are local and do not run NXT queries. Clear filters restores the combined view.

Constituent > **Key Indicator** supplies record type: I/Individual or O/Organization. It is matched by native field name and Constituent path, never by alias, donor name, constituency code or a spouse's type. Blank, unknown or inconsistent types remain visible in the combined view and a separate unclassified view; they are not silently treated as individuals or organizations. Missing type does not block history refresh or invalidate otherwise supportable dates. Add the optional output and refresh to classify previously untyped results. Conflicting types have an explicit review note and do not affect other donors.

The updated query criteria exclude Estate, Donor Advised Fund and Consortia constituencies. Refresh replaces the snapshot with the query's current results; the application neither restores excluded records nor adds name-based exclusions. Constituent-only exclusions do not incorrectly trigger the gift-history restriction safeguard.

Read-only validation after Key Indicator was added on November 16 returned 62,250 complete query rows, 402 distinct constituent lookup IDs, 233 individuals and 169 organizations, with no unknown types. The local parser produced 66 provisional dates and 738 threshold checks needing reconciliation. These are two threshold checks per record, not membership counts. No report snapshot was published and no donor records were changed by this validation.

## Source Outputs

Required output paths (aliases may vary; paths must match):

- Constituent > Constituent ID. Alternatively, Constituent > System Record ID if available, preferably aliased `Constituent System Record ID`.
- Constituent > Name.
- Constituent > Key Indicator (optional for import, needed for individual/organization views).
- Spouse > Spouse Information > Constituent ID and Constituent > Marital Status (optional for import, required with Key Indicator for automatic households). The actual November 16 output is named **Spouse ID** with native meaning **Constituent ID**. A direct Spouse path is also supported; unrelated nested relationship paths are not. Spouse system ID is supported only alongside constituent system IDs. Display aliases never determine ID meaning.
- Gift Information > System Record ID.
- Gift Information > Gift Date, Gift Amount and Gift Type.
- Gift Information > Soft Credit/Matching Gift Flag.

The actual November 16 metadata already has the required **Constituent ID**. No additional constituent system-ID output is needed for this read-only report. **Constituent Integration ID** was blank in all 51,453 returned rows; the output simply named **System Record ID** belongs to Gift Information. Neither is substituted for constituent identity.

When a constituent system-ID column is absent, the report groups by exact Constituent ID (lookup ID) within this query snapshot. Leading zeros and case are preserved. Internal keys are explicitly namespaced and are never API IDs. CSV system-ID cells remain blank; UI labels show Constituent ID. Imports and saved reads do not perform identity searches, NXT profile links or cross-report joins. Explicit linked checks can verify recipient profiles by system IDs returned on gifts; they do not persist a canonical crosswalk. Every refresh replaces the complete snapshot; group confirmations require unchanged source, member details and shared-gift evidence, never a lookup ID alone.

Missing identifiers, conflicting names for one identity, ambiguous output paths, or a lookup ID attached to multiple available system IDs stop publication and preserve the previous snapshot. Names never join different IDs. When the system-ID output is present, it is required on every row rather than silently falling back row by row.

The one-time read-only profile returned 51,453 rows, 18,530 distinct gift system IDs and 452 distinct constituent lookup IDs. These are diagnostic counts, not validated society membership counts. Multiple funds, installments and credit rows expand the result. The query filters on Donor Recognition descriptions for Kinne and 1934, excludes deceased constituents, credits soft gifts to Both/FullAmountToAll, and credits matching gifts to Both. It is not a discovery query for donors missing recognition attributes.

## Calculation Safety

- Whole gifts are deduplicated by constituent, gift system ID and credit flag, not by name or amount. Fund/installment output expansion does not multiply gifts.
- Conflicting repeated values or direct owners put affected constituents in review; unrelated records can still proceed.
- Same-day gifts are grouped before testing threshold crossing. The report does not invent within-day ordering.
- Recognized, uncomplicated direct and full-recognition gift types are calculated. Pledges, pledge payments, matching gift transactions and write-offs require a successful explicit linked-history check. Unsupported planned gifts, recurring commitments and unknown types remain in staff review.
- Full recognition is calculated only when query metadata explicitly says Both/Recipient and FullAmountToAll, and the row has a supported soft-credit flag. Other settings still require recipient allocation verification. Recipient names and soft-credit record IDs are not used to infer allocation. A donor's own gift is not added again for their personal soft credit. Conflicting full-gift values across recipients withhold affected histories and group suggestions.
- A supported-gifts subtotal is not a complete lifetime amount or proof of the milestone date. Conflicting gift values suppress even that subtotal. Complex histories continue to withhold complete totals and dates, rather than double-counting a pledge and its payments.
- Missing/invalid/future dates, invalid/negative amounts, unsupported society rules and gift-level filters withhold dates. An undemonstrated threshold is not a claim of ineligibility.
- Calculated dates remain **provisional** until explicitly confirmed by staff after reconciliation. Confirmed dates are labeled separately and retained without recalculating the crossing date; current gift-quality warnings remain visible. No official NXT membership totals are claimed.

## Access, Persistence And Operations

The registered dashboard defaults to specific-user access. Admins and Advancement Services manage source checks, refreshes, group confirmation/undo, saved dates, archive choices and linked checks; explicitly authorized viewers read saved snapshots. GET does not contact NXT. POST accepts only `fields`, `refresh`, `confirm-group`, `remove-group`, `confirm-date`, `reopen-date`, `archive-record`, `restore-record`, `check-linked` or `restart-linked` for fixed query 31118, checks active roles and same-origin requests, and redacts unexpected provider errors. Decision actions accept only an existing record/milestone hash and current revision, not arbitrary identities, dates, members or client-supplied totals. Linked actions additionally require a numeric gift ID present in the selected saved history; the client cannot submit paths or evidence. Refresh validates source readiness itself; a separate metadata check is optional rather than a required first step.

Refresh uses the existing shared reporting account and quota-aware transport. Complete CSV results are bounded to 32 MiB / 250,000 source rows / 10,000 constituents / 150,000 credit-gift keys; truncation is never accepted. The query job count must equal parsed rows. Query definitions and society rules are checked before and after execution. Failures leave the last snapshot unchanged. Publication uses revision compare-and-swap in existing `report_snapshots_cache` under `report:lifetime-giving-societies:v1`; no migration is required. Internal actor IDs are not returned.

Tests are part of the offline workflow gate: identity path validation, deduplication, threshold/day boundaries, conservative withholding, access control, row completeness, definition/rule races, stale revisions, source errors and viewer UI behavior. Fixtures are fictional.

## Remaining Calculation Limits

Unsupported historical amendments, planned gifts, recurring commitments and excessive or ambiguous linked graphs still need staff reconciliation. Current pledge balance and installment due dates alone cannot establish historical milestone dates. Full-gift recognition removes allocation uncertainty for this report, but does not prove historical completeness or marriage. Only reciprocal spouse IDs with Married statuses permit automatic household grouping; other eligible individual-only suggestions require explicit confirmation. A bounded live pilot should verify the new opt-in workflow after deployment before considering bulk checks or additional gift types.
